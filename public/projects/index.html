Insider threat & digital forensics: examination file access.

I conducted a digital forensic investigation into suspected unauthorised access to academic staff computers and examination materials at a university. The evidence sat in remote desktop activity, Windows security artifacts, user account information and endpoint events, so I reconstructed the access from those. The matter subsequently progressed to legal proceedings.

  • Examined Windows Remote Desktop Protocol (RDP) activity between laboratory and academic staff workstations.
  • Correlated Terminal Services events, including Event IDs 1027 and 1149, with account identifiers and remote connection activity.
  • Investigated administrator-account usage associated with access to academic systems.
  • Reconstructed a timeline of remote access and activity involving examination materials.
  • Correlated evidence across source and destination computers to build an evidence-based account of the observed activity.
  • Documented the technical findings.
